Analytics & privacy

Updated October 1, 2026

I use analytics to understand which projects people explore and improve this personal website.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” It uses cookies to distinguish browsers and sessions and measures page visits, scroll depth, visible sections, project details opened, demo starts and link clicks. This can show the sequence of actions within a visit; it does not tell me who you are.

The site does not send calculator inputs, form contents, email addresses or AI prompt text as analytics events. Page and destination URLs exclude query strings and fragments. Explicit UTM campaign labels are sent separately to measure referral campaigns. Please do not put personal information in campaign labels.

Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled in the site's tag configuration. There is no session recording. Embedded external projects have their own privacy practices; this site measures opening a demo, not interactions inside an external site's frame.

Choose “Analytics preferences” to decline or withdraw consent. Withdrawal stops this site's Google Analytics events and removes the Google Analytics cookies created on this host. Your preference is stored in your browser. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep optional analytics off.

Google processes analytics data under its privacy policy. This setup covers mj-kang.com and its local project pages; separately hosted subdomains may use different tools.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site and provides aggregate traffic and performance statistics. Cloudflare Web Analytics is configured to exclude EU visitors and operates separately from the optional Google Analytics choice. See Cloudflare Web Analytics for its measurement approach.
