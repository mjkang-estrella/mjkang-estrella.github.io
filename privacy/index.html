Anonymous counts & privacy

Updated October 2, 2026

This site counts pageviews and actions such as opening a project, starting a demo or clicking a link. It stores daily totals, such as “Block Fighter was opened 12 times today.” It does not connect those actions to a visitor.

What the counter stores

Each total has a UTC date, a known page, an action, a known target and whether it came from a full page or an embedded demo. The counter increments that total directly. It does not keep individual event records, precise event timestamps or a browsing history.

There are no visitor or session identifiers, fingerprinting, tracking cookies or session recordings. The counting request omits cookies and the referrer. Query strings, campaign tags, full destination URLs, calculator inputs, email addresses and AI prompt contents are not collected by this counter.

The site no longer loads Google Analytics. It removes the cookies created by the previous integration. If you previously declined that optional tracking, anonymous counts remain off in this browser.

Your preference

JavaScript is required for the counter and this preference control. With JavaScript off, no custom counts are sent.

The only preference stored in your browser is an optional on/off flag. It is not sent with count requests. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track also disable the custom counter.

Hosting and Cloudflare

The counter is hosted on Cloudflare and stores totals in a private D1 database. Its Worker request logging is disabled, and the counter does not read or store your IP address or browser information. Cloudflare still processes network information to deliver and protect the website, as it does for other web requests.

Existing Cloudflare Web Analytics provides separate aggregate traffic and performance reports and is configured to exclude EU visitor data. The preference above controls this site's custom counter. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and Web Analytics information.

External project demos have their own privacy practices. This site counts opening them, not activity inside their pages.
